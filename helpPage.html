How WargaReport Works

Everything you need to know about reporting civic issues, public countdown timers, and tracking city fixes.

Frequently Asked Questions
HOW IT WORKS How does WargaReport get city issues fixed?
  1. You spot an issue (pothole, water leak, dark street) and snap a photo.
  2. WargaReport verifies the evidence and dispatches it directly to the responsible municipal agency (PAM JAYA, Bina Marga, Dishub, DLH, Satpol PP, etc.).
  3. A public countdown starts. Neighbors can upvote the report to increase priority.
  4. Field crews resolve the physical issue and post verification proof before closing.
Privacy (UU PDP) Will my name or contact info be visible to the public?

Your personal contact details are kept private. Public reports show the issue information, location, and status—not your private account details.

ACCOUNT MANDATORY Do I need an account to lodge a report or interact?

You need an account to submit and manage reports, and to follow their progress.

ESCALATION What happens if an agency ignores or delays the report?

Overdue reports can be escalated and remain visible in the public tracking feed.

NOTIFICATIONS Can I get status alerts sent to my WhatsApp or Email?

Yes. You can choose to receive updates when your report changes status.

STATUS MEANINGS

What does each status mean?

Submitted Your report was submitted and received by our intake router.
Received Verified by WargaReport and queued for agency dispatch.
In review Agency coordinators are assessing field safety and equipment.
Assigned A field crew or technician is dispatched to the street.
Awaiting your info Inspector needs clarification or an updated photo.
Resolved Physical fix completed and inspection statement published.
Closed Case completed and archived.
Is this an acute life-safety emergency?

WargaReport is for non-urgent municipal infrastructure. If there is an active fire, medical crisis, or immediate hazard to life:

Call 112 (National Emergency) Call 113 (Fire Dispatch)
